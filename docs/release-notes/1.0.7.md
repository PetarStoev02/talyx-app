## Talyx 1.0.7

1.0.7 lets other AI agents operate Talyx, lets rooms change their bots after they are made, lets you keep typing while a bot answers, and fixes a long list of rough edges found in testing.

- **Talyx has an MCP server.** Any AI agent, a Talyx bot or an outside one, can now list, create and change bots, folders, rooms and routines through Talyx's own MCP server. It listens only on this machine and needs a key. Deleting something, replacing a SOUL, picking a costly model or starting a routine asks you first, in Talyx's window.
- **Change a room's bots.** A room's details panel can add and remove bots, rename the room and delete it. The room keeps its history.
- **Send while a bot answers.** You can send more messages while a bot or a room is answering. In a bot chat they wait under the answer, where you can send one right away, edit it or remove it.
- **Pick several roster rows.** Shift-click and ⌘-click (Ctrl-click on Linux) pick several bots, rooms or folders to drag into a folder, pin, mark as read, hide or delete together.
- **A sound when a reply finishes.** Settings → General has a Sound switch that plays a short chime when a bot finishes a reply. It is off unless you turn it on.
- **Clear endings.** A turn that ends without a reply, stops, or fails now says so and has a Retry button. While a model is rate limited, the chat counts down to the next attempt, and Stop ends the wait. The roster no longer shows raw error text.
- **A way back from Settings.** Settings has a Back button, and Escape, the gear and ⌘, (Ctrl+, on Linux) all return to where you were. Settings uses more of a wide window, and the details panel no longer squeezes the chat.
- **Smaller fixes.**
  - The model and reasoning menus work before your first message, and closing them puts you back in the text field.
  - Long clarify choices wrap inside their card.
  - Rooms show and accept @mentions by bot name.
  - A new bot's "What it does" fills its Description.
  - Quick drags into folders no longer get dropped.
  - Installing a module no longer reorders the Modules list, and Uninstall asks first.
  - On Linux, Alt no longer shows the menu bar and pushes the app down. Enter License Key is now ⌘⇧L (Ctrl+Shift+L on Linux).
  - Modules can mark their dock button when something waits on you.

## Install

- **macOS 13 or newer** (Apple Silicon: `Talyx-arm64.dmg`, Intel: `Talyx-x64.dmg`). Open the DMG, drag Talyx to Applications and open it.
- **Linux (x64).** Download `Talyx-x86_64.AppImage`, make it executable with `chmod +x Talyx-x86_64.AppImage` and run it. Or install the deb with `sudo apt install ./Talyx-amd64.deb`.

**Coming from 1.0.3 or later:** the update button shows "Talyx 1.0.7 is available"; click **Download**, then **Restart to update**. **Coming from 1.0.2 or 1.0.1:** Talyx downloads this update in the background on macOS and in the AppImage; click **Restart to update** or quit. A deb install links to this page. **Coming from 1.0.0:** click Download in the update button once.

## Check your download

`SHA256SUMS` lists the SHA-256 of every file in this release. Put it in the folder with your download and run:

- macOS: `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`
- Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`

Each file you downloaded should print `OK`. On a Mac, `spctl -a -vv /Applications/Talyx.app` should say `source=Notarized Developer ID`.

## The engine

Talyx 1.0.7 needs engine 0.22.7, the same as 1.0.6. To change a room's bots, Talyx adds a small plugin of its own to the engine's plugins folder; the engine itself is not changed.

# Code of Conduct

Talyx is a small project run by one person. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to keep it a friendly place to work.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what gets merged.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or report it privately to the maintainer, [@PetarStoev02](https://github.com/PetarStoev02), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people from the project who do not follow this code. This applies in every project space and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
